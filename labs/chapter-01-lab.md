# Chapter 1 Lab: AI Fundamentals: Concepts, Categories & Enterprise Use Cases

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Scenario-Matching Worksheet: classify 10 real business problem statements into the correct AI task category (language/vision/speech/recommendation/anomaly detection/forecasting) using Oracle's published AI use-case examples from Oracle MyLearn

## Key takeaway

AI is the broad concept of machines performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, and nearly every real-world deployment is narrow AI built for a specific task category.
