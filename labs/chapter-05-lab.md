# Chapter 5 Lab: Deep Learning Foundations Part 2: CNNs, RNNs, Transformers & When to Use DL

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Architecture Matching Quiz: match 8 data or task descriptions, such as 'classify X-ray images', 'translate a sentence', 'predict the next word in a sentence', and 'forecast a stock time-series', to the correct architecture family: CNN, RNN, or Transformer

## Key takeaway

CNNs suit image data, RNNs and sequence models suit sequential or time-series data, and transformers are the architecture behind modern large language models.
