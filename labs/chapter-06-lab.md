# Chapter 6 Lab: Generative AI & LLMs Part 1: Generative vs Discriminative, Tokens, Embeddings & Context

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Tokenization Walkthrough: manually split 3 sample sentences into approximate tokens and identify which words would likely share similar embeddings, using publicly available LLM concept diagrams from Oracle MyLearn generative AI materials

## Key takeaway

Generative models create new content rather than classify existing input, and LLMs process text as tokens represented by embeddings within a fixed context window.
