# Chapter 3 Lab: Machine Learning Foundations Part 2: Reinforcement Learning, Workflow & Evaluation

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Workflow Sequencing Exercise: arrange 6 shuffled ML lifecycle steps, data collection, feature preparation, training, evaluation, deployment, and inference/monitoring, into the correct order and match each to a one-line definition

## Key takeaway

The ML workflow moves from data collection through feature preparation, training, and evaluation to inference, and reinforcement learning improves behavior through reward signals rather than labeled data.
