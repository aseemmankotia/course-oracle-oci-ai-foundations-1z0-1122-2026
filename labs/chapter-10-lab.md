# Chapter 10 Lab: OCI AI Services Part 2: Document Understanding, Anomaly Detection & Forecasting

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Full-Stack Scenario Mapping: given a 6-step business process spanning invoice scanning, data extraction, equipment sensor monitoring, and demand prediction, map each step to the correct OCI AI Service, Document Understanding, Anomaly Detection, or Forecasting

## Key takeaway

OCI Document Understanding extracts structured data from documents, OCI Anomaly Detection flags outliers in operational data, and OCI Forecasting predicts future values from historical time-series data.
