# Chapter 9 Lab: OCI AI Services Part 1: Language, Speech & Vision

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Service Selector Drill: given 10 short task descriptions, such as 'detect the language of an email', 'count people in a security photo', or 'transcribe a customer support call', select whether OCI Language, OCI Vision, or OCI Speech applies

## Key takeaway

OCI Language handles text analysis and translation, OCI Vision handles images, and OCI Speech converts spoken audio into text, each with a distinct scope the exam expects you to distinguish precisely.
