# Chapter 7 Lab: Generative AI & LLMs Part 2: Prompt Engineering, RAG, Fine-Tuning & Agents

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Adaptation-Technique Decision Tree: given 6 business requirements, such as 'answer questions from our private policy PDFs' or 'always respond in a legal-formal tone', decide whether prompt engineering, RAG, or fine-tuning is the best fit

## Key takeaway

Use prompt engineering for quick guidance, RAG to ground responses in external or private data, and fine-tuning to adapt a model's style or behavior over time.
