# Chapter 8 Lab: Get Started with the OCI AI Portfolio

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Portfolio Placement Exercise: given 8 business needs, place each into the correct OCI AI portfolio bucket, pretrained AI Service, OCI Data Science custom model, in-database ML, or OCI Generative AI service, by reviewing the OCI AI portfolio overview page in Oracle documentation

## Key takeaway

OCI's AI portfolio spans ready-to-use AI Services, OCI Data Science for custom models, in-database ML inside Oracle Database, and the OCI Generative AI service, each suited to a different level of customization.
