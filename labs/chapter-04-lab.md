# Chapter 4 Lab: Deep Learning Foundations Part 1: Neural Networks, Layers, Weights & Activation

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Diagram Labeling Task: label the parts of a simple feed-forward neural network diagram, input layer, hidden layer, output layer, weight, and activation function, using Oracle's AI Foundations course diagrams as reference

## Key takeaway

Neural networks learn through layers of weighted connections and activation functions, and depth helps with complex, high-dimensional data but is not always necessary for simple structured problems.
